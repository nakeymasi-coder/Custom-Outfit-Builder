How to use

  1. Pick a preset or choose your own stacked options.
  2. Use the lock buttons before randomizing so your favorite parts stay put.
  3. Pick gender first so the men and women outfit, shoe, and accessory options stay clean and separate.
  4. Turn on the uploaded photo reference toggle when the user wants to upload a real photo and try the look on themselves.
  5. Generate the photo prompt or video prompt, save it, then use the extra variations button for three more versions.
